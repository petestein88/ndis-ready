Would your practice survive the interview?

Auditors don't sit and read your policy binder. They interview your workers and participants and ask "show me how this actually happens." This is that interview — answer the way your team honestly would.

"The auditor doesn't read the policies and tick boxes. They interview the worker. They ask the participant. When the documents say one thing and the practice says another, that's a non-conformity — even if the practice is genuinely good."

That gap — between what your documents say and what actually happens — is why capable providers fail audits. This simulator walks you through the questions a real auditor asks on the day, scores how each answer would land, and shows you exactly where your evidence falls short.
