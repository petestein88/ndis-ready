FAQ

Questions providers ask us

Straight answers about what the kit is, what it isn't, and how it helps you get audit-ready.

Will this kit guarantee I pass my audit?

No, and we'd never claim otherwise. The pass-or-fail call belongs to the NDIS Commission and your auditor, not to us. What the kit gives you is a complete, professionally written document set mapped to the NDIS Practice Standards, so you arrive organised and prepared instead of scrambling. You still need to complete each document honestly and accurately for your own service.

I'm not confident with computers. Can I still use this?

Absolutely. If you can send an email, you can use these. Every document opens in Microsoft Word or a free alternative like Google Docs, and you mostly just type your business name and details into clearly marked spaces. The 3 worked examples show you exactly what a finished document should look like, so you're never left guessing.

English isn't my first language. Will I understand it?

We've written everything in plain, simple English — short sentences and as little jargon as we could manage. Plenty of our customers run their services in a second language. If a document still feels unclear, the worked examples make it easy to follow, and you can always email us for a hand.

What if I buy it and it's not right for me?

You're covered by a 7-day pre-download refund. If you haven't downloaded your documents yet, just email us within 7 days of purchase for a full refund — no awkward questions. Because these are digital products that can be copied and kept, they're non-refundable once you've downloaded them — but you still get 30 days of email support to help you put them to work. This doesn't affect your rights under the Australian Consumer Law.

Is this legal or compliance advice?

No. These are professionally prepared templates and general information to help you organise your documentation — they are not legal advice. If you need guidance on your specific situation, please speak with a qualified professional.

When do I need to have this sorted by?

The 2026 SIL transition is already underway. From 1 July 2026, SIL providers are expected to meet new registration and Practice Standards requirements. If you're currently unregistered and delivering SIL, the NDIS Commission transition pathway indicates you may need to apply by 1 October 2026 to keep delivering while your application is processed. Starting your documentation now buys you more time to customise it, train your team, and gather your evidence.

Can I use this if I have no compliance background?

Yes. The kit is written in plain English and comes with worked examples to guide you. Just keep in mind you'll still need to tailor each document to reflect how your own service actually runs.

Can I claim this on tax?

Buying for your NDIS business? Many Australian businesses may be able to claim compliance-related digital products as a business expense where the purchase is directly tied to earning business income. Hold onto your invoice and check with your accountant — a tax invoice is provided with every purchase.
