Privacy Policy

Sacred Systems Pty Ltd  ·  ABN 23 690 792 655  ·  Last updated 30 May 2026

Sacred Systems Pty Ltd (trading as NDIS Ready) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and disclose your personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the 13 Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

1. What information we collect

We collect personal information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Your name and email address
  • Your organisation's name and ABN (if provided)
  • Your quiz responses (service types, organisation size, registration status)
  • Payment information (processed by Stripe — we do not store card details)
  • Communications you send to us by email

We also automatically collect certain technical data when you visit our website, including IP address, browser type, pages visited, and referring URL. This is collected via server logs and analytics tools.

2. Why we collect your information

We collect and use your personal information to:

  • Deliver your purchased compliance documents and access details
  • Send your free sample documents after quiz completion
  • Personalise your compliance checklist and results
  • Respond to your enquiries and provide support
  • Send you relevant compliance updates, product news, and reminders (you can unsubscribe at any time)
  • Improve our platform and understand how people use it
  • Meet our legal obligations

3. How we store and protect your information

Your data is stored securely in Supabase, a cloud database provider with servers located in the Asia-Pacific region. All data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. Access to your data is restricted to authorised personnel only.

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this Policy, or as required by law. You may request deletion of your data at any time (see Section 7).

4. Who we share your information with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with trusted third-party service providers that help us operate our platform:

  • Stripe — payment processing (their privacy policy: stripe.com/privacy)
  • Resend — transactional email delivery
  • Supabase — database and data storage
  • Vercel — website hosting and serverless functions

All providers are bound by confidentiality obligations and may only use your data to provide services to us. We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or regulatory authority.

5. Marketing communications

If you provide your email address, we may send you compliance updates, product news, and relevant information about NDIS registration. We comply with the Spam Act 2003 (Cth) — every marketing email includes a clear unsubscribe link. You can also unsubscribe at any time by emailing hello@ndis-ready.com.au.

6. Cookies and tracking

Our website may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to improve your experience and understand site usage. You can control cookie settings through your browser. Disabling cookies may affect some site functionality.

7. Your rights

Under the Australian Privacy Principles, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to legal retention obligations)
  • Complain about how we handle your information

To exercise any of these rights, email us at hello@ndis-ready.com.au. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Complaints

If you believe we have breached the Australian Privacy Principles, please contact us first at hello@ndis-ready.com.au. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au.

9. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this Policy periodically.
